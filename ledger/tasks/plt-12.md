## Reviewer notes and exact limits

- **The guard checks liveness at the start of a call only.** A call already open (a stream) continues after the lease ends, and the check and the call are not atomic. The README sentence that the guard refuses "every call once the child's registration is not live" must be read as every call that starts after that. Ending an open call is the owner's, through `LocalRpcRegistration.Ended`. The README was not changed in this task, so as not to move the reviewed head; fixing the wording is a follow-up edit.
- **A server's declaration and the router's declaration are tied only by the owner passing the same object** to `RequireDeclaredServices` and to `LocalRpcRouter.Add`; the library does not compare them.
- **The `contract_majors` in the child's endpoint manifest are not consulted** (the PLT.11 registration API takes none); the declaration is the parent's own knowledge and the router does not derive a service's majors from the launch's protocol version. A declaration says nothing about what the child binary really serves.
- **The guard has no method-to-capability map** (the capability check is the resolution) and does not follow channels built around it. Ended router entries stay until their slot is added again.
