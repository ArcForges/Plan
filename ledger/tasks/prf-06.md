- Interpretations, not design facts: the reading of "exponential full jitter from 0.5s to 30s", the probe's own bounds (5 minutes for a server retry time, 512 KiB received message, 64 remembered items) and the equality-only comparison of chunk and final hashes (their algorithm belongs to the Cloud producer; bytes are not verified against them).
