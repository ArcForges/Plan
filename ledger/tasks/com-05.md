## Owned elsewhere, limits and surviving findings (each with its owner or prerequisite)

- **No D1 store; in-memory substitute only (COM.16 by the narrowed graph).** `IEntitlementStore` has no production implementation. COM.16 (grant port, durable store; its planning pair is merged: Design `d89c1bc`, Plan `7dadc52`) owns the D1 store, the generic plan-execution port, the `entitlement.revision` write fence, the Commerce `IssueGrant`/`RevokeGrant` port and the GR-05 reason column (`entitlement.grant.reason`). CLOUD.03's physical manifest now contains the entitlement tables including `grant.reason` and `revision`; nothing in this task uses them. Deferred check: the same rebuild-equivalence proof against the durable store, restart included, prerequisite COM.16.
- **Requirement for COM.16: the `entitlement.revision` create precondition.** The service commits through `CommitAsync(workspace, expectedRevision, append, snapshot)` and treats a workspace with no stored row as revision 0. The durable store must create the workspace's revision row only through a guarded create (insert when absent, expected revision 0), so two first writers can never both commit; every later commit must advance the revision by exactly one under the fence, and records, definitions activations and the snapshot must commit in the same batch.
- **Store port carries grants, revocations and definitions activations only.** Terms, workspace status facts and feature releases are read as records; their owner admissions (COM.11 for terms and the others) must stamp the authoritative time as creation time and extend the port or share the unit of work before they can commit atomically with a snapshot. Until then a snapshot read from a store they appended to is stale until the next refresh and `VerifyRebuildAsync` reports a difference.
- **Single authoritative clock assumed.** The module registers `TimeProvider.System` per replica and the effective time never moves backwards past a stored computation, so one replica with a fast clock moves a workspace forward for every replica. The composition must supply one source or bound the accepted skew; the resolver does not.
- **History capped at 2000 records per workspace.** About 1.2 s replay at 1,700 records in Release on the review machine; there is no compaction path, so a workspace at the cap returns `InvalidHistory` permanently until a compaction design exists.
- **`QuotaExceeded`** is in the reason vocabulary but never assigned by the resolver (a usage fact owned by quota admission, COM.07). A lapsed paid term keeps a granted quota limit with `Available` by design; quota consumers must read the capability or the reason as well.
