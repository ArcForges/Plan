## Open follow-up against the PLT.13 call-bounds layer (not fixed here)

A client that stops reading multi-megabyte responses leaves the handlers finished, so no admission slot stays pinned and other peers are served, but the server holds one response per call in memory outside the 16 active / 64 queued bounds. Measured once locally: about 69 MB held and about 60 KB written to the wire for 16 calls of about 4 MiB. Reasoned, not measured: the bound is the 100-stream cap, about 400 MiB per connection and about 3.2 GiB over 8 connections, so PLT.13's recorded memory model (128 MiB per connection, about 1 GiB over 8) understates the worst case. A hosted test characterizes it with an upper bound only. It is not part of WP-08.06 and the fix would change `LocalRpcServer.cs`; it needs its own change against the PLT.13 layer. Streaming calls remain untested.
