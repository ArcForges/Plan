## Untested coverage and stated limits

- **No real streaming service.** Streaming was observed through the proof probe only; package-consumer streaming (C#, browser, Android) belongs to PRF.06 and CLOUD.29.
- **Revocation during a running stream.** A session and its owner are validated at admission only; a revocation reaches a running stream when it ends (at most about 5 minutes by design, 60 seconds for the probe). The 15 second re-check of annex 10 belongs to the first real stream service. Documented in `docs/cloud-ingress.md`.
- **Container keep-alive.** `sleepAfter` is 60 seconds and streams may last longer. The locked `@cloudflare/containers` keeps a Container with an open request awake and a test pins that library behavior; it was not observed on the platform (the longest observed stream lasted about 2.6 seconds).
- **Bearer credentials** are refused as unauthenticated: no `IBearerTokenVerifier` implementation exists until the identity module.
- **No directly reachable Container port** is established by configuration and static tests (no route, no published port, one binding caller) and by the provider receipts that show only the Worker routes, not by an external probe.
- **`IsCanonical`** treats an empty raw target as unknown (see the review note above).
- **Not separately observed on Cloudflare:** provider limits under load, trailer handling beyond the one custom trailer, and the cold-start bound with a really unavailable Container (covered offline with mocked timers).
- **Not claimed:** the deployed run does not make the proof probe a product API, and a deployment or a passing scenario is not commercial acceptance. PLT.48's completion edge on CLOUD.01 asks for a real Cloud hop; the correlation propagation across it is PLT.48's own follow-up.
