## Limits stated plainly

- A binding whose owner operation does not come from `Enforce` is gated by the authorize step only and skips the owner's final validation, the result record and the audit event; the invocation pipeline cannot be asked to refuse it. A product composition must build every binding through the gate.
- The audit event is written after the owner's effect. If the sink fails, the effect stands with no audit trace; the invocation reports `internal.unexpected` (`BookkeepingFailed`, effect `Happened`).
- A product cannot compute the effect digest (the algorithm is private); an approval for the exact effect is obtained by presenting the refused request.
- Step-up proofs are bound to the command identity, not to the effect digest (inherited from PLT.38).
- Refusals before the decision pipeline (no evidence, a throwing or slow evidence source, a malformed identity or origin) write no security audit event; the invocation trace still records them.
- Whether the evidence (actors, scope, resource and revision) is true is the host's fact, and the gate does not check that the chain's device or installation is the target's.
